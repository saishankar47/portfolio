This portfolio should grow alongside my career.

As new technologies, certifications, and projects are completed, the portfolio should evolve without requiring major architectural changes.

The codebase is designed to be:

- Modular
- Reusable
- Scalable
- Well documented
- Easy to customize
- Production ready
- Enterprise quality

The ultimate objective is to maintain a portfolio that accurately reflects my professional growth and serves as a polished showcase for recruiters, hiring managers, and technical peers.
